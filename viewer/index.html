Compare an area

Enter WGS84 bounds, or choose “Draw rectangle” and click two corners on a map. Partial cells use spherical area fractions and assume uniform population within each cell.

Coverage is the spherical area fraction with count data, including zero-count cells. “No coverage” is distinct from zero people. Estimates are rounded for display; exports retain numeric precision. Differences are not confidence intervals.

Compare a local count GeoTIFF

For another supported area, download a source TIFF yourself and choose it here. The worker reads a native window of at most 262,144 cells, with at most 32,767 pixels on either display axis. Only single-band, north-up, PixelIsArea EPSG:4326 stored counts are accepted. Rotated/projected files, internal masks/alpha, scale/offset, invalid counts and very large decode blocks are rejected. External .msk files are not supported. No file is uploaded.

WorldPop and GRID3 downloads currently lack browser CORS and ignore byte-range requests. Local files and these prepared same-origin excerpts avoid that limit. GPW is not bundled; its current Harvard Dataverse access uses a guestbook.
