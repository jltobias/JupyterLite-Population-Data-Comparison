Coverage is the spherical area fraction with count data, including zero-count cells. “No coverage” is distinct from zero people. Estimates are rounded for display; exports retain numeric precision. Differences are not confidence intervals.

Bring a small count grid

Import rectangular WGS84 GeoJSON cells with source metadata. Counts only; maximum 2 MB, 2,000 nonoverlapping cells, 5° per cell. A layer’s declared synthetic status is carried into its label and exports.
