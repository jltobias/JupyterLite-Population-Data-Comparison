Explore linked grids, examine their assumptions, and recompute the comparison in your browser. A teaching workspace inspired by POPGRID Viewer and the work of NASA, SEDAC/CIESIN, and the POPGRID Data Collaborative.

SYNTHETIC TEACHING DATA. All bundled counts are invented. They are not observed population or any GPW, WorldPop, LandScan, HRSL, GHS-POP or WPE product.
