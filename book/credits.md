## Material actually bundled

**Synthetic teaching cells:** JupyterLite-Population-Data-Comparison contributors (2026), *Invented comparison cells*, version 1.0. Generated by deterministic arithmetic on 2026-11-06; reference year 2020 is an invented scenario label. No actual census, imagery, basemap, or population data inputs. Fixture data are dedicated under [CC0-1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The <a href="../data/README.md">data record</a> records generation and checksums; each layer has its own metadata. Teaching counts are not any of the six historical population products.

**Software:** Project Jupyter and JupyterLite contributors; Pyodide contributors; Jupyter Book and MyST contributors. Their distributions retain upstream license notices. The viewer bundles [Leaflet 1.9.4](https://leafletjs.com/), copyright Vladimir Agafonkin and CloudMade, under its <a href="../viewer/vendor/leaflet/LICENSE">BSD-2-Clause license</a>. No external basemap tiles are used. Original project code is <a href="../LICENSE">MIT licensed</a>; that license does not relicense third-party software, cited research, or future data assets.

**Illustration:** the original AI-generated project splash remains unchanged with its <a href="../assets/README.md">generation record</a>. Its geography and marks are invented; it is not a screenshot or analytical result.
